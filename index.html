About the data

Scores are fantasy totals. Averages are fantasy points per player game. Injury status is current. Check availability in ESPN before adding a player.

Standing rank is the shared record. Power rank is separate. On a completed week, power and luck are through that week.

All-play counts a result against every other team in a finalized week. Pythagorean luck compares wins with the score-based expectation through that week.

The future-game count is the fantasy schedule. It is not games left on a roster.
