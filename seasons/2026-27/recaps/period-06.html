About the data

Win probabilities are not published.

Scores are the fantasy totals for each week. Power rank is separate from standing rank.

The future-game count is the fantasy schedule. It is not games left on a roster.

power_score = 0.625 * normalized season points-for/points-against + 0.375 * normalized matchup-period points. Strength of schedule is not a component.

Totals use matchup 6 and earlier finalized matchups only.

Averages elsewhere on the site are fantasy points per player game. Injury status is current.

Weekly scoring rank orders teams by fantasy points in that matchup. Ties share a rank.

A streak counts consecutive finalized matchups with the same result. A missing matchup or a tie ends it.

Points versus the week average compare a team with the mean score of every team in that matchup.

Average opponent power rank is the mean power rank of the next fantasy opponents. Lower means stronger. The ranks come from the finalized week named on the line.

Highest-scoring loss and lowest-scoring win use that matchup only. A high score that loses is not called unlucky.
